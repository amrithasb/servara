Preview status

Servara 1.0 is provided as an unsigned technical preview for evaluation and demonstration. It has not completed code signing, notarization, independent security, privacy, accessibility, or legal review.

Appropriate use

Use fictional or low-risk information. Keep original documents and an independent backup. Do not treat the preview as the sole source of critical information or deploy it on a computer you do not trust.

No professional services

Servara organizes user-entered references and instructions. It is not a password manager and does not provide legal, financial, tax, medical, insurance, estate-planning, or emergency services. It does not create legal authority or execute transactions.

No recovery promise

The application cannot recover a lost master password or Trusted Access passphrase. Loss of credentials and usable backups may permanently prevent access to encrypted information.

The technical preview is provided for evaluation without a promise that it is error-free, fit for a particular purpose, continuously supported, or appropriate for sensitive production data. These plain-language preview terms are not a substitute for professionally reviewed release terms.
